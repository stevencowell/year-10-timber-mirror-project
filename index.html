Project Sequence

Mirror Build Pathway

Follow the project from safe setup and design planning through to construction, finishing, mirror fitting and evaluation.

01

Plan and Prepare

Read the drawing, identify requirements, prepare a cutting list and plan safe, accurate work.

 02

Design Brief

Choose a user, sketch ideas, set size limits, plan features and record design choices.

 03

Materials and Cutting List

Select timber, measure accurately, prepare a cutting list and minimise waste.

 04

Mark, Cut and Dress

Mark out parts, cut safely, plane or sand faces, check square and keep evidence photos.

 05

Joinery and Dry Fit

Prepare joints, test the frame dry, adjust for square and record problems before gluing.

 06

Assembly and Finishing

Glue and clamp, sand through grits, apply finish safely, fit mirror/backing and evaluate.

Learning Blocks

Theory Notes

Use the theory notes to build the knowledge behind the mirror: planning, timber, measuring, joinery, routing, finishing, fitting and evaluation.

Open theory notes

Plan and Prepare

Read the drawing, identify project requirements, prepare a cutting list and plan safe, accurate work before cutting timber.

Timber Materials

Source weeks: 4, 7, 9, 11, 12 and 13. Grain, defects, seasoning, milling, conversion, cost, yield and sustainability.

Measuring and Marking

Source weeks: 1 and 2. Accuracy, datum edges, square lines, cutting allowances and checking before cutting.

Joinery

Source weeks: 4, 5 and 6. Dowel, biscuit, domino and mortise and tenon joints, including strengths and limits.

Routing and Profiles

Source week: 7. Router safety, profile choices, rebates, round-overs and test cuts on scrap timber.

Finishing

Source weeks: 10, 12, 13, 15 and 16. Sanding, oils, wax, varnish, low-VOC finishes and safe rag disposal.

Design Process

Source weeks: 2, 9 and 17. Design brief, constraints, sketches, features, user needs and communication.

Mirror Fitting

Source week: 14. Glass/mirror handling, rebates, backing, hardware, safe fitting and industry links.

Evaluation

Source weeks: 17, 18 and 20. Quality checks, feedback, self-assessment, final reflection and skill transfer.

Student Evidence

Portfolio Structure

The portfolio content from the old site is now organised around what students actually need to collect and explain.

Open your interactive evidence folio

Required Evidence

  • Student name, class and project title.
  • Design brief and target user.
  • Sketches or design photos with short notes.
  • Materials list and cutting list.
  • Process photos for key workshop stages.
  • Problems, fixes and teacher feedback.
  • Final evaluation against quality, function and appearance.

Evidence Flow

  1. Plan the mirror and record constraints.
  2. Photograph marking out and cutting stages.
  3. Record joinery tests and dry-fit checks.
  4. Show sanding and finishing progression.
  5. Add final product photos and evaluation.
